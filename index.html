What it does

Everything your crops need to thrive

Six sensors, one intelligent platform — giving you complete visibility and control over your irrigation.

💧
Soil Moisture Sensing
Continuous measurement of volumetric water content at root depth, triggering irrigation only when needed.
🌡️
Temperature Monitoring
Dual soil and air temperature tracking to account for evapotranspiration rates and optimal root zone conditions.
🌫️
Air Humidity Tracking
Real-time atmospheric humidity monitoring to factor evaporation losses into precise irrigation calculations.
🧠
AI Irrigation Engine
Smart algorithm combining soil type, crop variety, growth phase and climate to compute the optimal water dose.
📱
Smartphone Control
Monitor all metrics and control irrigation remotely from any device — anywhere, any time.
⚠️
Smart Alerts
Instant notifications when moisture drops below critical thresholds, preventing crop stress before it starts.
Process

From sensor to smart watering

Four steps to complete irrigation intelligence.

01
Install the Sensor
Place S.sensor in the root zone of your crop. It connects to your Wi-Fi in under 2 minutes.
02
Configure Your Field
Set your soil type, crop variety, growth phase and irrigation system in the app.
03
Real-Time Analysis
S.sensor streams live data to our engine, which calculates the optimal irrigation dose continuously.
04
Automated Irrigation
Your irrigation system activates automatically — or you approve every dose manually. Your choice.
